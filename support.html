Common questions

How do I get consistent scans? Same room, same steady light, flash off, dry hair with no product. Use the timer or ask someone to take the top and crown photos.

How do I cancel my subscription? On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Hairpath. You can also restore a purchase in Hairpath under Settings, Premium.

How do I delete my data? In Hairpath, open Settings, Privacy and data. You can delete a single scan, turn off AI analysis and delete the cloud copy, or delete everything.

Is Hairpath a medical device? No. Hairpath helps you track your hair over time. For diagnosis or treatment, see a dermatologist.
