Hairpath: Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version

Everything stays on your iPhone until you turn on AI analysis and backup. That feature asks first, on a screen that explains what is sent. You can turn it off and delete the cloud copy at any time.

There is no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking. Hairpath does not sell your data.

Stored on your iPhone

Your preferences, your scan photos, the AI readings of them, your routine and its checklist, and your reminder choices.

If you turn on AI analysis and backup

Hairpath creates a private cloud account for this iPhone. It has no name, email or phone number.

Uploaded to Hairpath's cloud storage (hosted by Supabase): the scan photos you save, a smaller copy of each for analysis, the analysis results, and a backup of your journal (your scans, their readings and your routine checklist). Location and other photo metadata are removed before upload. Only your account can read them.

Sent to our AI service provider for each analysis: the smaller copy of the photo, the smaller copy of your starting photo for the same view when there is one, which view they show and the days they were taken. The provider processes them only to produce the reading. Under its commercial terms it does not use this content to train its models, and it deletes it within 30 days unless it must keep it longer for safety or legal reasons.

Your subscription is checked with Apple: the App Store's signed purchase record is sent to Hairpath's server so it can confirm an active subscription. It contains no payment card details.

The server keeps short technical logs (what happened and when, with no photo content) for up to 90 days to keep the service working and to prevent abuse.

Never uploaded

The sample journal, unfinished scans and your reminder settings.

Deleting

Deleting a scan removes its photos and readings from this iPhone and, if they were uploaded, from the cloud.

Turn off and delete cloud copy removes the cloud account and everything stored under it, and keeps the journal on this iPhone.

Delete all my data removes everything on this iPhone and in the cloud. If the server can't be reached at that moment, uploads stop immediately and the cloud copy is deleted automatically as soon as it can be.

A cloud account that hasn't been used for 13 months and has no active subscription is deleted automatically, with everything stored under it.

Deleting can't remove files you already exported or shared, or copies inside device backups you made earlier.

Device backups

Your iPhone's own backup, such as an iCloud or computer backup, may include Hairpath's data depending on your device settings. Unfinished scan photos are marked to stay out of backups.

Permissions

Camera access is requested only when you choose Open camera. Choosing a photo uses the system photo picker, which gives Hairpath only the photo you select. Notifications are requested only when you turn on a reminder; reminders are scheduled on the iPhone, not sent from a server.

Children

Hairpath is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Contact
