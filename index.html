Hairpath

See what your hair is really doing. Scan your hairline, top and crown with three photos, get an AI reading of each area, and follow your routine.

Every few weeks, take the same three photos with on-screen outlines. Hairpath gives you a Hair Score, compares each scan with your first one area by area, and marks the day your routine started on your progress chart.

Your scans, readings and routine build one journal on your iPhone. Share a progress image with your dermatologist whenever you like.
