Hairpath: Terms of use

Last updated 20 November 2026

What Hairpath is

Hairpath is a personal journal for tracking how your hair looks over time, with AI analysis of your scan photos. It is intended for adults.

Hairpath does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. AI readings describe what a photo appears to show; they can be incomplete or wrong, and they don't say what causes hair loss or whether a treatment works. For sudden or patchy hair loss, a sore, red or scaly scalp, or any worry about your health, ask a qualified healthcare professional.

Premium subscription

Hairpath Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's App Store, monthly or yearly. The price for your country or region is shown before you confirm.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically for the same period at the price shown unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your Apple Account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Without an active subscription, scans you already saved stay readable, exportable and deletable, and you can keep taking scans on your iPhone. AI analysis, comparisons and backup need a subscription.

Your content

Your photos and routine are yours. Hairpath uses them only to provide the app to you: to show them, to back them up if you turn that on, and to produce the AI analyses you ask for. You can export or delete them at any time.

Only add photos of yourself. Don't add photos of other people without their agreement, and never of children.

Fair use

AI analysis has daily and monthly limits per person so the service stays available for everyone. Don't try to get around the app's limits or use the service for anything other than your own hair journal.

Sample content

The sample journal uses illustrated pictures, not photos of a real person. Guide articles are general information and have not been reviewed by a healthcare professional.

No warranty

Hairpath is provided as it is. To the extent the law allows, it comes without warranties, and its makers are not liable for decisions made on the basis of the journal or an AI analysis.
